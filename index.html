Baseball, the way

Meet the teams ↗

The South Sound Premier League is an 18U summer league for Tacoma and Pierce County. Games are played on Tuesday and Thursday nights at Peck Field, Lincoln, and Fort Steilacoom. Seven to ten games a summer, no league fee, and weekends kept open for tournaments. It is run by the coaches whose teams play in it.

STEP 01

There is no league fee.

Teams keep their budgets for their own programs.

STEP 02

Each team hosts its home games.

The home team covers umpires and field time for the games it hosts, and every team carries its own field insurance.

STEP 03

Games are midweek.

Seven to ten games across the summer, played primarily on Tuesday and Thursday nights. Weekends stay open for tournaments.
